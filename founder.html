Meet Our Founder

The vision, heart, and lived experience behind MoveMinds.

Founder & Executive Director

Maya Reynolds

Maya is a dance educator and advocate with over a decade of experience working alongside neurodiverse children and their families. Her journey began as a movement therapist, where she witnessed firsthand how dance could unlock confidence in children who struggled to find it elsewhere.

Driven by a belief that every child deserves to feel the joy of moving freely, Maya founded MoveMinds to build spaces where difference is not just accepted — it's celebrated.

Get in Touch
“

I started MoveMinds because I saw children light up the moment they were given permission to move in their own way. That spark is what we protect every single day.

— Maya Reynolds, Founder
