Meet Our Founder

The vision, heart, and lived experience behind MoveMinds.

Founder & Executive Director

Uditha Harish

Uditha Harish is the founder of MoveMinds, a youth-led initiative dedicated to creating inclusive adaptive dance experiences for neurodiverse children. A lifelong dancer and student leader, she founded MoveMinds after recognizing the need for movement programs where every child feels welcomed, supported, and empowered. Through her experience in dance and community service, Uditha has worked to make creative movement more accessible for children of all abilities. She believes that dance is more than performance—it is a way to build confidence, encourage self-expression, strengthen social connections, and create a sense of belonging. Beyond MoveMinds, Uditha is passionate about neuroscience, research, and community advocacy. She hopes to combine her interests in medicine, scientific research, and service to develop innovative solutions that improve the lives of individuals with neurological and developmental differences. Her vision for MoveMinds is to build a community where every child has the opportunity to experience the joy of movement, discover their potential, and know they belong.

Driven by a belief that every child deserves to feel the joy of moving freely, Maya founded MoveMinds to build spaces where difference is not just accepted — it's celebrated.
