About MoveMinds

We believe every child deserves the freedom to move, express, and belong — exactly as they are.

Our Story

Born from a belief in every child

MoveMinds began with a simple observation: when neurodiverse children are given the space to move on their own terms, something remarkable happens. Walls come down, smiles emerge, and confidence grows.

What started as a handful of weekend sessions has grown into a community of families, educators, and volunteers united by a shared belief — that movement is a universal language of joy and belonging.

Our Mission

To empower neurodiverse children through adaptive dance and movement programs that foster confidence, creativity, and meaningful connection.

Our Vision

A world where every neurodiverse child has access to inclusive, joyful movement — and where difference is celebrated as strength.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

Inclusion First

Every program is designed so that no child is left on the sidelines.

Joy in Movement

We lead with play, celebration, and the pure delight of moving together.

Family & Community

We support the whole family and build belonging beyond the studio.

Respect for Difference

We meet each child where they are and honor how they uniquely move.

Creativity

We nurture imagination and self-expression in every session.

Lasting Impact

We build programs that create real, measurable change over time.
